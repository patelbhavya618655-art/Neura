NEURA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · App: NEURA (com.neura.ai) · Developer: Patel Bhavya

1. Who we are

NEURA is an AI chat application developed by Patel Bhavya. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

2. Information you provide

When you use NEURA you may type messages, attach images or text files, create AI agents, add items to the knowledge base, and use voice input. This content is used only to give you the response you asked for.

3. AI processing

Your messages and any attachments you send are transmitted over HTTPS to our AI service provider so that a reply can be generated. The provider processes the request to produce the answer. Please do not send information you would not want processed by a third-party AI service.

4. What is stored on your device

Chat history, your agents, knowledge base items, settings and preferences are stored locally in the app on your device. NEURA does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

5. Images, files and voice

Images and files you attach are sent to the AI service only for the request in progress. Voice input uses your device's speech recognition; NEURA does not store your audio recordings. Microphone and camera permissions are requested only when you use those features, and you can deny them.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location, or device identifiers for tracking.

7. Children

NEURA is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the app.

8. Accuracy of AI answers

AI-generated responses may contain mistakes or outdated information. Please verify important information before relying on it.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The updated date at the top will always show the current version.
